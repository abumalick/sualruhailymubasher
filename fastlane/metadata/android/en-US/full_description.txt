Listen to Sheikh Abdur-Razzaq Al-Badr's lessons in the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah: the live broadcast during the lesson, and recordings of past lessons, each with its place in the book being explained.

• Live: a notification when the lesson starts, and listening inside the app.
• Recordings: every lesson with its title, its place in the book and its page number, to review any time.
• Preparation: roughly where the next lesson will start, so you can read it beforehand.
• Notifications: choose the lessons you follow, and what each one sends you: broadcast start, recording available, preparation reminder.
• Current lessons: the explanation of Tafsir as-Sa'di after Fajr, and the commentary on the explanation of Kitab at-Tawhid after Maghrib.

No account needed, no ads.

This is an unofficial app made by students of knowledge. It is not affiliated with the Sheikh or his website. The lessons come from the Sheikh's website al-badr.net and its live broadcast.
