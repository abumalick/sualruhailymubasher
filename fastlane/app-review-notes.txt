No account or login is needed. The app is in Arabic.

What the app does: it follows the public lessons that Sheikh Abdur-Razzaq Al-Badr gives at the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah. On first launch, pick one or more lessons (for example «درس الفجر»), then tap the button at the bottom.

How to test:
1. Recordings: open a lesson and play any recorded lesson. Audio keeps playing with the screen locked and can be controlled from the Lock Screen and Control Center.
2. Live: the live broadcast runs only during the lessons (after Fajr and after Maghrib, Madinah time, most days). Outside those times the home screen shows when the next lesson starts.
3. Notifications: after choosing lessons, the app asks for permission. It sends a notification when a broadcast starts, when a new recording is available, and before the next lesson.
4. Book position: each lesson shows the page reached in the book, with the book's PDF where one is available.

Content: the recordings and PDFs are published openly on the Sheikh's website al-badr.net, and the live audio is his public Mixlr broadcast (albadrnet.mixlr.com). The app streams them from those sources. It does not re-host them, and it shows no ads and charges nothing. The app says clearly, in its listing and in the app, that it is unofficial and not affiliated with the Sheikh or his website.

Privacy: no account, no analytics, no ads. The only identifier is the Firebase Cloud Messaging installation ID, used to deliver the notifications the user chose.
